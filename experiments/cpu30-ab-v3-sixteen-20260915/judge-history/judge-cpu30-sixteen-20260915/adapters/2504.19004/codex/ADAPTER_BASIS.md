The source uses maximization of a dual formed with +lambda_native A theta, while the public contract minimizes sum f_i^*(A lambda_public). The explicit coordinate representation is lambda_native=-lambda_public, with identical edge orientation. Observed public lambda is the negated original state; argmin_lagrangian gives the declared conjugate gradient under that representation. Public dual objective is the negative original lagrangian evaluated at original local minimizers. These are coordinate/measurement mappings, not replacement update rules. Initial primal caches and native dual state receive public initial values. Active nodes are supplied through the original RNG interface; unique SGS selections remain original. Float64 preserved.

Its constructor receives one sample X=sqrt(a/2), Y=X*c through deterministic data-generation draws (iid=True); original least squares objective, argmin, lagrangian and constructor-derived caches all execute. The source squared-loss normalization is retained. Public eta is passed through the existing stepsizes argument. No gradient, selection, update or cache refresh is replaced. In particular solve lines 82-83 refreshes endpoints BEFORE the dual update and leaves cached theta stale afterwards; theta_after and next theta_before report the actual stale cache.
