# Paper-only basis: 2504.19004

Source is the original benchmark PDF with SHA256 in contract. PDF p.8 was rendered and Algorithm 2 visually checked; p.4 dualization, pp.5–6 recurrences and definitions were read directly from the PDF text. No reproduction workspace, adapter, prior report or observed outputs were accessed.

The tested contribution is optimization-aware **setwise** neighbor selection, SGS-CD. The dual objective is Eq. (3), p.4: F(lambda)=sum_i f_i^*((A lambda)_i). With the provided quadratic centers and positive curvatures, the conjugate is exactly c_i y + y²/(2a_i) and its gradient is c_i+y/a_i. Equation (6), p.6, produces each edge gradient using its two endpoints. Algorithm 2, p.8, selects the largest absolute gradient only among the active node's incident edges, performs the single-coordinate Eq. (4) update, and refreshes conjugate-gradient caches at the two endpoints.

Two cases each contain four serial node activations. The triangle case has a cycle and nonzero initial dual coordinates; the path case includes active sets of different sizes and events where only locally accessible coordinates may be chosen. Both have nonuniform curvatures, nonzero center differences and unique choices, so neither tie-breaking nor orientation is left implicit. Node and edge indices, incidence signs, exact activation realization, step size and observation timing are public. Cached conjugate gradients are the specified theta observables; no extra primal descent process is invented.

All computations are exact Fraction arithmetic, including dual objective values. The fixed 1e-12 absolute/relative tolerance permits float64 serialization and arithmetic over four events. The step 1/8 is conservative under the direct Hessian bound ||A||²/min a <= 4. These cases test unweighted SGS-CD's dual mechanics, local selection and updates. They do not test SU-CD, coordinate-wise Lipschitz extensions, concurrent conflicts, theoretical rates, or latency. Expected values are independent mathematical derivations, not reproduction outputs.
