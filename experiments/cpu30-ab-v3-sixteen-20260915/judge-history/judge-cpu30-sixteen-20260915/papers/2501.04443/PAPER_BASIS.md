# Paper-only basis: 2501.04443

The input PDF is version 3 dated February 24, 2025. Source identity is bound by the contract SHA256. PDF p.5 was rendered and visually checked for Eq. (4) and every line of Algorithm 1. Only the original paper and contract schema informed this work.

This paper contributes analysis of existing algorithms. It explicitly defines LocalSGD in Section 3.2, pp.4–5, Eq. (4), and a simplified SCAFFOLD in Algorithm 1 on p.5. The latter is materially different from persistent control-variate SCAFFOLD: it freezes each worker for tau stochastic gradient evaluations, averages this anchor batch, performs tau−1 corrected local updates, and uses line 14 for the final synchronized update. Its anchor gradients are recomputed every cycle. The paper itself describes these simplifications above Algorithm 1.

The LocalSGD case includes four microsteps and two synchronization events. The SCAFFOLD case includes eight microsteps, two frozen anchor phases and two training/aggregation phases. Worker Hessians and offsets differ, and public additive-noise realizations are nonzero. Inputs explicitly define the sample objective, the iid bounded noise law and conditioned draws, initial states, full participation, eta, tau, and observations before and after the microstep. This prevents mistaking the other paper's persistent control variates for this paper's variant.

All expected values are exact rational recurrences computed by Python Fraction, with float conversion solely for JSON. Absolute and relative tolerance 1e-12 was chosen before observations and accommodates float64 arithmetic over these short trajectories. No reproduction code, prior evaluation, or observed output was read. The scope is recurrence correctness, including synchronization and anchor refresh; it does not test the improved convergence rates or certify all alternative similarity assumptions used in the theory. Unequal-curvature quadratics need not satisfy a global uniform gradient-similarity bound.
